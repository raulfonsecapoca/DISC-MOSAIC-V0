Mechanical Support Design in SolidWorks (C2-CE3)

Justification for the competency

Justification

As part of the MOSAIC project, I was responsible for designing the mechanical support structure for our optical system using SolidWorks. The goal was to create a robust, precise, and manufacturable design that aligns with the project's optical and functional constraints.

This required multiple iterations of modeling and simulation, ensuring that the structure provided necessary stability while minimizing weight and material usage. I also had to consider real-world manufacturing constraints such as available materials, machining limitations, and assembly feasibility.

This experience strengthened my ability to work with CAD tools, understand mechanical design principles, and integrate engineering constraints into practical solutions.
