Working in a Diverse Team (C7-CE1)

Justification for the competency

Justification

One of the most valuable aspects of the MOSAIC project was working in a highly diverse team composed of members from different nationalities and cultural backgrounds. Our team included three French, three Chinese, and one Brazilian (myself), creating a rich environment for collaboration.

This diversity required effective communication, cultural awareness, and adaptability to different working styles. Despite language and cultural barriers, we successfully coordinated our tasks, shared responsibilities, and made collective decisions, ensuring the progress of our project.

My role in this context involved not only contributing technically but also fostering collaboration among my teammates, ensuring that our differences became an asset rather than a challenge.
