Teamwork and Task Distribution (C7CE1)

\evel 3 competency

Justification

In the MOSAIC project, teamwork was structured based on both competencies and individual preferences. Initially, we divided the team into two subgroups: Zemax and MATLAB, allowing each member to focus on their preferred area. As part of the MATLAB group, I contributed to image processing tasks.

In the second week, when we needed to implement an Exponential Phase Mask, I shifted my focus to this task due to my familiarity with C++. This approach was mirrored across the team, with each member concentrating on activities aligned with their strengths.

With a large (7-person) and diverse team (three nationalities, different academic backgrounds), we effectively allocated tasks to individuals with the most relevant experience. This structure ensured efficiency, fostered collaboration, and allowed us to integrate various technical skills into the project.
