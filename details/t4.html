Resources (C7CE4)

Level 2 competency

Justification

To ensure the successful execution of the MOSAIC project, we identified and utilized key resources, including:

  • Human resources → Team members specialized in different areas, as well as the professors responsible for our group, who provided guidance.
  • Technical resources → Zemax, MATLAB, and programming tools.
  • Organizational resources → Planning prévisionnel, Journal de bord on eCampus, and OwnCloud for file sharing.

This structured resource management allowed us to optimize collaboration and efficiency throughout the project.
