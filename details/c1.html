Using Simulation and CAD (C2-CE1)

Justification for the competency

Justification

In the MOSAIC project, I played a key role in developing and applying computational simulations to optimize optical designs. My work involved the use of MATLAB to implement a Wiener filter-based convolution, improving image quality by reducing noise and enhancing sharpness. This process required a deep understanding of signal processing and numerical simulations.

Additionally, I worked on CAD modeling in SolidWorks to design mechanical supports for optical components. This involved translating theoretical designs into practical, manufacturable models while ensuring alignment with project specifications and constraints.

These activities highlight my ability to bridge the gap between theoretical models and real-world applications using simulation tools and CAD software.

Proofs

  • MATLAB Simulation Report - Analysis of convolution with Wiener filtering.
  • CAD Model for Optical System - 3D design of the mechanical structure.
